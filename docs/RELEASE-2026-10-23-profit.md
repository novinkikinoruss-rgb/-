# Profit and overview — OSNO, VAT 22%

The new Profit section filters by customer, object, service and period. Overview is the initial workspace and presents financial result, expense distribution, estimated VAT, payroll/company tax entries and contractor balances. The original customer, fleet, contractor and trip workspaces remain accessible.

Revenue uses delivered, nondeleted app-scoped trips, the trip's volume and customer price. Expenses use their recognition date. Profit is net revenue minus recorded costs after confirmed recoverable input VAT. Margin is profit/revenue, undefined for zero revenue. Services are grouped by customer/object/service/unit; tonnes and cubic metres are never added together. Missing payroll, contractor trip links, unknown VAT settings, unconfirmed input VAT and unallocated overhead are visible.

Payroll, bonuses and employer tax come from the existing immutable accrual/payment/deduction ledger. Fine withholding reduces driver payout debt, not the original gross payroll cost. Fuel, repairs, leasing, insurance, tolls, company tax and other costs are entered in an owner-isolated expense journal. Expense records support edit, optimistic version checking and soft deletion. Company income/property taxes are actual entered accruals; no unconfirmed profit-tax rate is applied.

Contractor invoice amounts are included once as gross costs, even when unpaid. A journal allocation assigns part of an existing invoice to a customer/object/service/trip, without adding another full invoice cost. The unallocated remainder stays visible as overhead. Invoice payment amounts are apportioned across allocations by largest remainders, preserving every kopeck. The report explicitly calls these payments/balances current snapshots of the selected expenses, not cash transactions during the selected period. Invoice amounts cannot be reduced below allocations, and allocated invoices cannot be reassigned or deleted until allocation is corrected.

User confirmed OSNO and 22% VAT. Whether the existing trip prices include VAT remains a separate explicit setting. Until specified, net profit/margin/VAT payable are not presented as final; the UI asks to clarify inclusion. The configuration applies to this app's financial report and does not rewrite trip/specification prices. Included VAT uses gross×22/122; excluded VAT is added to net. Input VAT requires a document rate and explicit confirmation of invoice/UPD and deduction eligibility. Unknown/unconfirmed input VAT is not deducted. Contractor VAT metadata is stored once per invoice and shared by its allocations. Salary and tax payments have no input VAT. The tax block is an estimate from selected operations, not a VAT declaration or automatic refund; advances, tax-period adjustments and actual tax return calculations are outside this release.

Database migrations in order: profit_workspace.sql → profit_vat.sql → profit_expense_vat.sql. Earlier app-isolation and trip releases remain prerequisites. All new finance tables have owner RLS and no direct client write privileges; validated RPCs perform changes with pinned search paths and explicit app-scoped reference checks. The original shared CRM records are not adopted.

Validation: pure calculation tests cover accrual profit, VAT22 included/excluded, unknown VAT, confirmed input deduction, invoice conservation/rounding, filters, mixed units, salary/tax/fines and zero-revenue losses. Authenticated SQL rollback integration covers journal CRUD, relationship derivation, VAT metadata, stale records/profile rejection, invoice over-allocation guards and owner/application isolation. Deployment/build/type validation and browser limitations are recorded in the release status.
